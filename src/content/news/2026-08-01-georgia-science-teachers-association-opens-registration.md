The 2027 GSTA Annual Conference will be held February 11 through February 12 at AmericasMart, 240 Peachtree Street NW in Atlanta. Early bird registration is listed at $200, with standard registration at $250, a $50 rate for pre-service teachers using a school issued email, and $150 options for single day attendance on either Thursday or Friday.

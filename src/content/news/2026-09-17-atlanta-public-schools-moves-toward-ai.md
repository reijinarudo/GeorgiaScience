---

Under the draft policy, teachers could use AI tools to help plan lessons but would be barred from entering sensitive information into AI platforms. Students could use AI only when its use connects to a clearly identified instructional objective, and could not use it to complete assignments or generate their work. Administrators are scheduled to decide whether to adopt the policy at a meeting in November.

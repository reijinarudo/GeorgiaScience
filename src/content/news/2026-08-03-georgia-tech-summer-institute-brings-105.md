---

The Georgia Tech Summer Institute, run by Expanded Learning Programs within CEISMC, selected its 2026 cohort from more than 200 nominations submitted by school districts across the state. Students attended sessions on admissions, financial aid, and student life, and took part in hands-on activities led by faculty from several Georgia Tech colleges, all at no cost to participants.

Read the [source](https://ceismc.gatech.edu/georgia-tech-summer-institute-creates-pathways-possibility).

Programs that give Georgia students hands on exposure to STEM fields and campus research environments are one piece of the broader effort to build science literacy across the state.

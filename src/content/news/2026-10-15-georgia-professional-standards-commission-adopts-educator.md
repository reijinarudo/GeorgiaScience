summary: "The Georgia Professional Standards Commission has adopted rule changes to its educator preparation standards, effective October 15, 2026, including updates to three programs that add requirements from the Math Matters Act (HB1030)."
---

The Commission initiated rulemaking on July 9, 2026 and adopted the changes on September 10, 2026, with an effective date of October 15, 2026. Among the changes, rules governing the Elementary Education (P-5) Program, the Mathematics Education Program, and the K-5 Mathematics Endorsement were each updated to add requirements set forth in the Math Matters Act, HB1030, which the notice says passed in the 2026 legislative session. The same notice also covers unrelated changes, including new artificial intelligence endorsements for educators and updates to certification rules for provisional, international exchange, and military support certificates.

Read the [source](https://www.gapsc.com/Commission/Rules/Current/FinalNotice/Final%20Notice%20of%20Effective%20Rules_10-15-26.pdf).

Changes to how future teachers are trained in mathematics shape how Georgia students will encounter science and math concepts in the classroom.

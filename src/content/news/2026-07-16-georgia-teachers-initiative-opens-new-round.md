Through the Georgia Teachers Initiative, the Georgia Foundation for Public Education is offering between 500 and 2,500 dollars to Georgia public school teachers for projects they describe as innovative. The current application round runs from July 16, 2026 to October 15, 2026, with winners to be announced by November 15, 2026. Teachers who have already received a Teacher Grant for Innovation award are not eligible to apply again.

The hub offers a free course called AI Literacy for All and a teaching guide from UGA's Center for Teaching and Learning. This fall the university also expanded a student pilot program that lets participants try ChatGPT Edu, Gemini Pro for Education, and Microsoft 365 Copilot, and the Institute for Artificial Intelligence at UGA unveiled a new Certificate in Artificial Intelligence, Language, Minds, and Machines.

Read the [source](https://news.uga.edu/uga-expands-ai-resources-for-campus-community/).

Resources like this hub can help Georgia students and educators build practical AI literacy as the technology becomes part of everyday coursework and research.

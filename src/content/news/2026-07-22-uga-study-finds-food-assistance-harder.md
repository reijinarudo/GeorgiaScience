The Georgia Hunger Study also found that a lack of reliable transportation is a major barrier for many food pantry users. Researchers surveyed and held focus groups with more than 2,000 food pantries across the state, working with UGA Cooperative Extension, the Georgia Department of Human Services and Feeding Georgia, and the team recommends better coordination among food banks, expanded transportation help, and stronger links to social services.

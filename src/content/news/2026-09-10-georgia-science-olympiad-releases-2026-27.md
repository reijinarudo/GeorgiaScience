The schedule lists invitational tournaments across Georgia starting with the Georgia Scrimmage on October 17-24, 2026, and continuing through the Yellow Jacket invitational in Atlanta on February 6, 2027, for both Division B and Division C teams. Regional tournament dates and sites are expected by early October, with team sign-ups opening November 4 and running through December 11. State tournaments are planned at the University of Georgia for Division B and Georgia Tech for Division C, with specific dates not yet listed, and organizers say the 2027 state tournaments will be split into two tiers based on team size and experience.

Read the [source](https://www.georgiascioly.org/home/competitions).

Statewide science competitions like these give Georgia students a structured way to practice hands on scientific and engineering skills outside the classroom.

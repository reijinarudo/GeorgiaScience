According to the Atlanta Journal-Constitution, Powell received nearly 2.3 million votes, about 386,000 more than Woods, in the November 3, 2026 election. Powell, a first-time political candidate, said she plans to assess systems within the state Department of Education, talk with district superintendents, and visit the state's lowest-performing schools before taking office. She also said she will work on expanding Georgia's pre-kindergarten program and on updating the state's school funding formula, which was enacted in 1985.

---

The 52,018-square-foot building will include five classrooms, 14 teaching and research laboratories, faculty offices, and flexible collaborative spaces. The project drew on $39.3 million in state funding secured by state Sen. Steve Gooch and state Rep. Will Wade, along with a $3 million gift from alumni Stewart Swanson and Carol Barnette Swanson.

Read the [source](https://ung.edu/news/articles/2026/07/ung-launches-bold-vision-for-future-of-stem.php).

A dedicated STEM facility with more classroom and lab space is a concrete marker of how Georgia institutions are investing in future scientists and engineers.

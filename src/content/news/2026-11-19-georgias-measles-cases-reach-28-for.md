The Georgia Department of Health reported 28 confirmed measles cases in the state so far in 2026, nearly triple last year's total and the most since the state began tracking cases in 2002. Three of the latest cases, confirmed in Macon County on November 10, were close contacts of an unvaccinated resident diagnosed with measles in October, and the national rise in cases has led the United States to lose its measles elimination status, a Pan American Health Organization designation the country had held for 26 years, amid what the report describes as inconsistent messaging on the MMR vaccine from U.S. Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention states that the MMR vaccine is safe and effective, with serious reactions extremely rare.

Georgia Milestones tests students in grades 3 through 8 and in identified high school courses in math, science, and social studies each year. The 2025-2026 results, released August 6, showed gains or steady performance across nearly all of the 13 tested subjects, with math posting some all-time highs. English Language Arts results are still pending because the state administered newly designed ELA assessments for the first time this year.

Original announcement: [Georgia Department of Education](https://gadoe.org/press-releases/georgia-milestones-scores-continue-to-show-improvement-with-some-all-time-highs-in-math/).

Steady or rising science proficiency on state assessments is one indicator, among many, of how well Georgia's K-12 system is building the scientific literacy the state will need.

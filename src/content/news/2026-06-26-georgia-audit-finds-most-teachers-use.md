The Georgia Department of Audits and Accounts surveyed all public K-12 classroom teachers in the state from March 16 to April 6, 2026, receiving 13,679 responses from teachers in 150 of 180 public school districts. The performance audit, numbered 26-03, found that teacher use of generative AI was driven mainly by time savings and improved instructional materials, while concerns about accuracy, academic dishonesty, and reduced critical thinking grew as teachers reported higher levels of student use, particularly in middle and high schools.

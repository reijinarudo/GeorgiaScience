---

The camps are funded through a grant from Google and cost nothing for participants. Sessions are scheduled in cities including Macon, Augusta, Columbus, Fayetteville, Alpharetta, Athens, Atlanta, Suwanee, McDonough and Decatur, running on different dates from June through July 2026.

Read the [source](https://gafirst.org/news-resources/de-ghjjl).

Free, statewide access to hands-on coding and robotics instruction for middle schoolers is the kind of early exposure that can shape science literacy across Georgia.

Agnes Scott College added a two week AI unit to its mandatory seven week course for incoming first year students this fall, covering how AI works, its ethics, and when it can be used in coursework. Georgia Tech and Georgia Gwinnett College have added AI components to required computing classes, and Georgia Southern University now has incoming students complete a 90 minute online 'AI fundamentals' course as part of a push to incorporate AI across every academic program. The push comes as a University of Georgia report found student cheating accusations nearly doubled in the 2024-25 school year, with just over half of the cases alleged to involve AI.

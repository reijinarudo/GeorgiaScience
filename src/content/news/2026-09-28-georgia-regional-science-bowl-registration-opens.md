According to the Department of Energy's registration page, coaches can begin creating accounts and submitting school preregistration starting October 5, 2026 at noon Eastern, with team registration due by November 23, 2026. The subregional competition takes place February 6, 2027, and the top teams from each subregion advance to the state championship on February 12, 2027 at the University of West Georgia's Education Center in Carrollton. The event allows up to 30 teams, a maximum of two per school, and carries a $60 registration fee.

Read the [source](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/nsb/Regional-Competitions/High-School-Regionals/Georgia/GA_Georgia-High-School-Regional-Science-Bowl).

Events like the Georgia Regional Science Bowl give the state's high school students a chance to test their science and math knowledge in competitive team format.

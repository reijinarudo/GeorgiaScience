The fair will be held at UWG's Carrollton campus for students from Carroll, Coweta, Douglas, Haralson, Heard, Meriwether, Polk, or Troup counties, or homeschool groups in those areas. Among the mandatory registration steps, the university now flags a new AI policy for reviewing, linked out to Georgia Science and Engineering Fair documentation, but the specifics of that guidance are not described on this page. The university calls the regional fair a gateway to the state and international competition pipeline.

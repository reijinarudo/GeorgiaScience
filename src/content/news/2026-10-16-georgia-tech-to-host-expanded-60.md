Georgia Tech's Center for Education Integrating Science, Mathematics and Computing, known as CEISMC, facilitates the FIRST LEGO League Challenge for the state and operates the State Championship event held at Georgia Tech. The site states that Georgia Tech will host an expanded 60 team state tournament on February 20, 2027, during the 2026-2027 season, which the page calls the BIOGLOW season. Regional competitions are set for November and December with Super Regional competitions in January, ahead of the February state tournament.

Read the [source](https://fll.ceismc.gatech.edu/home).

Statewide robotics tournaments like this one give Georgia students a hands-on entry point into science and engineering.

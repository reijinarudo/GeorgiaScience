The playbook was developed alongside a new Academic AI Strategy and is led by David Joyner, Georgia Tech's interim vice provost for AI in Education. It grew out of a campus wide effort to understand how instructors were already using AI, and the first edition covers examples of faculty using AI to support instruction, teaching students how AI is shaping their fields, and redesigning assignments for classrooms where AI tools are now common.

Read the [source](https://news.gatech.edu/news/2026/08/31/academic-ai-strategy-establishes-shared-vision-teaching-and-learning-georgia-tech).

The playbook is a Georgia-produced resource that educators elsewhere can look to as AI use in classrooms becomes a live question in science and technical education.

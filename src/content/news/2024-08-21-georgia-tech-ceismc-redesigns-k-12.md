Georgia Tech's Center for Education Integrating Science, Mathematics and Computing redesigned its summer professional development for K-12 InVenture Prize teachers, adding peer led learning, hands-on activities, tours, and technical training. Workshops for middle and high school teachers met at Georgia Tech in Atlanta with IronCAD instruction and a tour of a campus makerspace, a separate elementary teacher workshop met in Atlanta with a paper-making session, and further workshops were held at Southern Regional Technical College in Southwest Georgia and in Savannah, all open to past, current, and new K-12 educators.

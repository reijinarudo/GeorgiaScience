The State Board of Education met on September 23, 2026, and approved SBOE Rule 160-4-2.39, Advanced Mathematics Requirements. The report describes the rule as a new one meant to ensure alignment with the provisions of HB 1030, the Math Matters Act. The source does not describe what the rule's specific requirements are.
